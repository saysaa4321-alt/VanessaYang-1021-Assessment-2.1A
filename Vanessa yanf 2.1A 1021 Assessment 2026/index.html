Project Statement

Intent

This is a pitch and organised compilation of what will be included in the website I am building. The website portfolio aims to reflect my vibrant visual style and creative process as an artist.

Audience

The website will be catered towards illustration and design companies as well as freelancing hires.

Tone

I intend my portfolio to emotionally attract its viewers through conveying a balance of bright and carefree atmosphere with an underlying safe and soft feel.

Analysis of Precedent Portfolio

Yukaidu

The website’s visual style is dominated by the colourful and adventurous atmosphere through the art works and design shown, which contrasts the clean and contemporary composition of the website. It is really effective in making the website visually pleasing and balanced for its users.

Best Aspects

  • There is an animation to present the artist’s name
  • her works are presented in a way which makes the site feel cohesive colour wise
  • Very clean and not afraid of leaving empty space. Allows attention to be focused on the works
  • Good use of columns and grid layout within the images and body texts

From my observation the site primarily uses warmer colours such as warm whites, pale pinks, darker pinks, and darker oranges. The lighter colours are used for the backgrounds and the darker colours are used for elements that need to standout or headings.

The site only utilises two type faces. The typefaces used also enhance the contemporary feel.
Body Text : San-serif fonts are used likely due to its simplicity and usability. Emphasised parts in the body text are shown through being bolded.
Headings and Quotes : Are all in a serif font, however ranges in size accordingly.
